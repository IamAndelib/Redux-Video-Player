## Research
- **Upstream:** VLC's CI (`extras/ci/gitlab-ci.yml`) runs `meson test` with `VLC_TEST_TIMEOUT: 60` and
  `-Dextra_checks=true`; it has no QML lint. `test/dump_stacktraces.sh` prints backtraces of crashed tests (autotools
  only); its idea (gdb in batch mode) is what found G24's cause.
- **Prior art:** requirement IDs written into the source documents are standard traceability practice. Doorstop and
  StrictDoc are open-source tools for it, but both want to own the requirements files, which would duplicate the
  brief and SCOPE.md; a YAML case list plus a small validator/report script (PyYAML is in the image) is the KISS fit.
  QML warnings: VLC already routes `qml`/`js` console messages into its log (`util/vlcqtmessagehandler.cpp`), so a log
  scan sees them. qmllint stays for later (QA.md).
- **Reused:** `shoot.sh`'s Xvfb + xdotool way of running the real app, moved into `redux/tools/lib/app-session.sh`
  and shared; `play-matrix.sh` for L2; meson's `testlog.json` for L3 results.
- **New gaps:** G26 (a bit flip in the host's RAM). G25 resolved (load-induced timeouts; the upstream timeout).
