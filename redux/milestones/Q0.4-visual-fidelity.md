## Research (T1)
Already in place:
- **Isolated run profile (from M0.3):** done. `redux/dev.sh` gives every container run a throw-away `HOME`
  (`/tmp/home`), and `dev.sh app` keeps its own profile in `build/app-home`; the host's VLC settings are never touched.
- **Figma references:** the three scenes `shots.tsv` compares (first-run `1899:3055`, library `970:3308`, player
  `187:328`) are in `redux/design/source/frames/` (fetched through the Figma connector on 2026-10-24). The `.fig`
  decodes offline with fig2sketch's Kiwi reader (MIT; see the M0.2 issue), so exact values (colours, type, radii)
  don't need the API.

Researcher report (T1):
- **Region comparison: ImageMagick 7 (in the image) with openQA's needle format** (os-autoinst, GPL-2: the format
  idea only). Per scene a JSON list of areas (`xpos`, `ypos`, `width`, `height`, `type` match/exclude) plus our
  `name` and regression threshold. `magick compare -metric DSSIM -read-mask <mask>` compares a crop with its masked
  pixels left out (verified: the score is averaged over the unmasked pixels only). DSSIM, not AE (AE returned
  fractional counts and ignored `-fuzz` in 7.1.2). Rejected: Playwright, odiff, pixelmatch, BackstopJS, reg-suit (new
  dependencies for what ImageMagick does); Qt's lancelot (GPL-3/commercial, client-server).
- **Contrast: a palette dump from the running app.** `SystemPalette::getColor()` already applies the real fallbacks
  (state → Normal, set → View, magenta when missing), so a small new `style/palettedump.cpp` walks the `QMetaEnum`s
  (set × section × name × state) when `REDUX_PALETTE_DUMP` is set; a QtTest would need most of the module (MainCtx).
  The report composites translucent colours (axe-core's approach, idea only) and applies WCAG 2.x: text 4.5:1 (1.4.3),
  focus, borders and indicators 3:1 (1.4.11, 2.4.11); disabled colours listed but exempt.
- **Screens in the KWin session:** VLC's title bar and shadow are its own QML (CSD), unaffected by
  `QT_WAYLAND_DISABLE_WINDOWDECORATION`. Scenes are driven through AT-SPI (the Q0.3 driver) instead of xdotool, and
  captured with `kwin-shot.py`. Needs a render node, so L6 runs locally, not on GitHub's runners (G27); the Figma
  frames are local-only anyway.
- **Multi-track clip:** FFmpeg, 2 Opus audio and 2 SRT subtitle tracks with languages and 3 chapters from an
  ffmetadata file (verified with ffprobe).

## Plan (T2)
- **T3 clip:** `make-media.sh` adds `Videos/Clips/tracks-demo.mkv` (60 s, 640×360, 3 chapters, English and French
  audio and subtitles), for the tracks, chapters and subtitle milestones (M2.4, M5.x).
- **T4 scenes in KWin:** `shoot.sh` keeps its interface (`[scene…]`, `THEME`) but runs the scenes as AT-SPI driver
  scripts (`redux/qa/visual/`) inside `kwin-session.sh`, the window maximised at 1920×1080, captured with
  `kwin-shot.py`; the library scene opens the folder the Figma frame shows. A `window` scene shows the window inset
  (title bar and shadow). Xvfb leaves `shoot.sh`.
- **T5 regions:** `redux/qa/visual/regions/<scene>.json` (needle-style areas, excludes for the clock, time labels
  and video picture); `compare.py` writes per region the DSSIM to Figma (a progress score, never pass or fail) and
  to the committed baseline crop (`redux/qa/visual/baselines/`, pass or fail against the region's threshold).
  `compare.sh` becomes a wrapper. QA check `l6.<scene>.<region>`, skipped without a render node.
- **T6 contrast:** `style/palettedump.cpp` (new, in `meson.build` and `Makefile.am`) plus a 2-line hook in
  `systempalette.cpp`; `redux/qa/contrast.py` writes `contrast.md` with every pair in Night and Day; QA check
  `l7.contrast`, report-only (non-blocking) until M1.1 sets the new palette.
- **T7:** baselines into `STATUS.md`, cases, docs (D33), QC.
