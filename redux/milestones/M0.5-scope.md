## Research
Three inventories and one community survey, 2026-10-20:
- **UI features** (`modules/gui/qt`): about 150 user-facing features in 18 groups, each traced to its file. Not in the
  Qt UI at all: AtmoLight, a sleep timer, ratings; 360° navigation lives in the core.
- **Modules** (`modules/`, `meson_options.txt`, the build log): 422 plugins built today. a52, dca and libmpeg2 are
  gone upstream (avcodec covers them). `vdpau`, `gstdecode` and `notify/` build only with autotools.
  `-Dstream_outputs=false` drops only `mux/`, encoders and Chromecast; `stream_out/` and `access_output/` still build,
  so M0.6 needs meson guards. FFmpeg gives about 95% of codec coverage; `postproc` is absent with FFmpeg 8.
- **Roadmap mapping:** no milestone covered codec or format support; the cut candidates were network, music,
  conversion, recording, extensions, casting and profiles.
- **Community** (VideoLAN forum, upstream issues via the GitLab API, Lemmy/Reddit mirrors, Flathub's
  `org.videolan.VLC` manifest):
  - Essential: plays anything, fast seeking, tracks and ASS subtitles, subtitle sync, resume, auto-next in the folder
    (upstream #22382), hardware decoding, speed, volume above 100%, HDR tone-mapping.
  - Cut candidates: YouTube and site scripts (upstream deleted `youtube.lua`, commit `cd94dcaec9`), online services,
    renderers, sout/VLM/remote interfaces, capture and broadcast, skins, Lua extensions (VLSub breaks with each
    OpenSubtitles change), novelty filters.
  - Flathub VLC is VLC 3.0.23 on Qt 5 with its own FFmpeg 4.4.8 and libplacebo 0.2.1 without Vulkan; it ships
    libdvdcss/libaacs; open issues: AACS key path (#317), AV1 no video (#198), Opus no audio (#275).
- New gaps: G21 (app ID ownership), G22 (formats the matrix can't synthesize).
