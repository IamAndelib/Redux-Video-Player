## Goal
Asked for by the maintainer (2026-10-24): check automatically, on any machine with a GPU, that the player decodes
video on the GPU where the GPU supports the codec, falls back cleanly where it doesn't, and plays without dropped
frames or errors. Until now this was one hand check in M0.7 (Intel iHD, inside the Flatpak).

## Research (T1)
Probe on SKYNET (AMD RX 5500, Navi 14, Mesa 26.2.5 radeonsi), in the dev container:
- `vainfo` decodes H.264 (CB, Main, High), HEVC Main and Main10, VP9 profiles 0 and 2; no AV1 (RDNA1).
- `--vout=dummy` can't take GPU frames: VLC logs `vout: none found` after `trying format vaapi` and silently decodes
  in software. A real check needs an OpenGL output.
- In `kwin-session.sh` with GPU OpenGL (without `LIBGL_ALWAYS_SOFTWARE`), `--vout=gl` played 4K HDR10 HEVC and
  1080p H.264 through VA-API: `vaapi generic: Using Mesa Gallium driver …`, `4cc VAOP` in the video output format,
  `using glinterop module "glinterop_vaapi"`; 0 dropped or late frames, 0 errors. No crash: #73 needs software GL with
  a GPU visible.
