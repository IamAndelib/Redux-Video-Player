## Goal
Remove the UI entry points of every feature `redux/SCOPE.md` cuts (D14), at one model or menu place each, without
deleting upstream files. The list is in `redux/design/screen-map.md` § Feature trim. Media Information stays
reachable. Acceptance (ROADMAP): no cut feature is reachable from any menu, toolbar or shortcut; Qt tests pass.

**Decision (maintainer, 2026-10-24, D36):** the Browse section stays until M3.1 brings folder browsing to Videos
(brief D5). Removing it now would leave no way to browse folders: stock VLC 4's Video section has only "All" and
"Playlists".

## Research (T1)
A researcher (entry points) and a test writer (D35 step 1, "generate") worked in parallel. Findings:
- **One builder serves every menu:** the menu bar, the top bars' "Menu" buttons and right-click menus all come from
  `VLCMenuBar::*Menu` in `menus/menus.cpp`, so removing an entry there removes it everywhere.
- **But a menu entry's key is only a label.** The shortcuts are `ShortcutExt` items in
  `menus/qml/GlobalShortcuts.qml` (Ctrl+D, N, C, R, S, M, T, Ctrl+Shift+W). VLC's core hotkeys (Shift+R record,
  W wallpaper, Shift+M disc menu, X/Shift+X program, Ctrl+Shift+V secondary subtitles, the boss key) live in
  `modules/control/hotkeys.c`.
- **Entry points the screen map missed:**
  - the play queue's right-click "Stream", "Save" and "Advanced Open" (`qml_menu_wrapper.cpp`)
  - the subtitle panel's "Search online" (VLSub) and "Select multiple" (dual subtitles)
  - the Open dialog's Disc, Network and Capture tabs and Stream/Convert actions (`dialogs/open/open.cpp`)
  - "Close to systray"
  - the Interfaces submenu (Console, Telnet, Web…)
  - Home's "New Music" row; the no-library Home's "Open Disc" button
  - the visualization and lyrics buttons in `Player.qml`
- **Toolbar:** the control list in `PlayerControlbarControls.qml` both lists the editor's controls and resolves saved
  IDs. A missing ID renders a fallback with a warning. The stock profiles contain cut controls (Renderer, Navigation,
  Record), and the current player baseline shows the cast button. Filtering the cut IDs in `ControlListModel` covers
  stock profiles, saved profiles and the editor at one place; IDs are never renumbered.
- **Already inert (compiled out or module cut):** VLM, update check, skins, Lua extensions and VLSub.
  **Still live:** Convert/Save and Stream (their dialogs open even with stream output off), EPG, Messages, Program,
  Renderer, Go to time, 3D, Wallpaper.
- **Prior art:** none outside VLC needed; upstream already has `QTabWidget::setTabVisible`, the control-list filter
  and the compile guards.
- **Maintainer decisions (D37):** keep Ctrl+V paste (it plays files copied in a file manager); keep the error
  popup's "Show Details" until M8.3's "Save a log"; hide Home's "New Music" row now; leave the novelty filter tabs to
  M5.3, which rebuilds that panel.

- **T3 tests (RED):**
  - `test_navigation_model.cpp` expects Home, Video, Browse (Home, Browse without the library).
  - A new `test_control_list_model.cpp`: cut IDs are dropped when a profile is loaded or a control inserted.
  - L5 `redux/qa/e2e/test_trim.py`, which first probes that the QWidget menus are visible over AT-SPI:
    - every menu without a cut entry, with Media Information present;
    - the cut shortcuts open no window, with Ctrl+I as the positive control;
    - the toolbar editor without cut controls, and an old profile still loading.
- **T4 navigation and Home:** remove Music and Discover; hide "New Music"; the no-library "Open Disc" button.
- **T5 menus:** `menus.cpp`, `qml_menu_wrapper.cpp`, the Open dialog's tabs and actions.
- **T6 shortcuts:** `GlobalShortcuts.qml`; `hotkeys.c` ignores the cut actions (D37).
- **T7 toolbar:** `player/cut_controls.hpp` and the `ControlListModel` filter; the QML list entries go.
- **T8 the rest:** system tray never created and its settings hidden; the visualization and lyrics buttons;
  Media Information read-only (no Save, no Fingerprint, no cover-art menu).
- **T9 QA and docs:**
  - L6 re-baselines for the sidebar (Music, Discover gone) and the controls (cast button gone)
  - cases and hand checks in `EVALUATION.md`
  - the QA loop, review, CI
  - after the merge, the Flatpak on the maintainer's host (the first major milestone)
